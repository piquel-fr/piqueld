# Code of conduct

piqueld follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
in its issues, pull requests, discussions, and every other place its
community meets.

In short: be welcoming and respectful, assume good faith, give and take
feedback on the work rather than the person, and don't harass anyone.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to
[contact@piqueld.dev](mailto:contact@piqueld.dev). Reports are kept
confidential, and the maintainers act on them as the Covenant's enforcement
guidelines describe.
